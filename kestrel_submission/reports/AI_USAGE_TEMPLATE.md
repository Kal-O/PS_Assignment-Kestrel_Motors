# AI Use Log — Kalpana

AI was used primarily to assist with structuring the project workflow, exploring potential approaches to consolidating data from multiple Excel and CSV files, and organising the presentation of the business findings. It also supported the development and review of technical logic for tasks such as identifying inconsistent source formats, handling date conversions and exploring rule-based sentiment classification. AI suggestions were treated as guidance rather than automatically accepted solutions.

I independently reviewed source files, spot-checked 52 sources, examined data-quality issues and checked analytical results against the processed data. This helped identify limitations such as inconsistent workbook templates, source-specific date formats and differences in rating-scale direction. The sentiment classifier was also evaluated against a small human-labelled sample, achieving 25% exact agreement across 16 reviewed comments. This highlighted the need for further validation before relying on automated sentiment classifications.

AI was also used to help structure the final report and communicate the findings, recommendations and limitations clearly. I reviewed the reported customer satisfaction trends, department-level results and illustrative financial estimates against the underlying analysis and assumptions. I retained responsibility for interpreting the evidence, deciding which findings were sufficiently supported, and ensuring that recommendations acknowledged uncertainty. Overall, AI supported the technical exploration, structure and documentation of the project, while the final analytical decisions and conclusions remained my responsibility.
